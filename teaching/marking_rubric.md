# Marking rubric. Mapping sealed and green surfaces in a Scottish town

Four criteria, marked against the usual UK bands. The overall mark is the weighted sum of the criterion marks.

| Criterion (weight) | 70 and above | 60 to 69 | 50 to 59 | 40 to 49 | Below 40 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Data and processing (25%)** | Well-justified choice of scenes and season, correct cloud masking and reflectance scaling, features chosen and explained with insight | Sound choices, correctly processed, features explained | Mostly correct processing with minor errors, limited justification | Processing works but contains errors that affect the results | Processing incorrect or missing |
| **Map and cartography (20%)** | Clear, accurate map with legend, scale, north arrow and source credits, designed for its reader | Clear map with all essential elements | Readable map with some elements missing | Map hard to read or missing several elements | No usable map |
| **Validation and interpretation (35%)** | Random and blocked results compared and explained with reference to spatial autocorrelation, limitations of the labels discussed critically, a defensible figure recommended | Both validation designs reported and the gap explained | Both designs reported with limited explanation | Only one design reported, or the explanation is wrong | No meaningful validation |
| **Reproducibility and communication (20%)** | Notebook runs cleanly, code is tidy and commented, report is concise and well structured within the word limit | Notebook runs with minor issues, report is clear | Notebook needs small fixes, report is understandable | Notebook does not run without significant fixes, report is unclear in places | Notebook missing or report incoherent |
