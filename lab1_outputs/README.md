# Saved Lab 1 outputs

These two files are the training pixels and training squares produced by Lab 1 on 8 November 2026. Lab 2 downloads
them when it cannot find a local copy, for example when it is opened on its own in Google Colab. If you run Lab 1
yourself, Lab 2 uses your own files instead.

- `lab1_training_pixels.csv` holds 3,340 pixels with their class, square, coordinates and the eleven features.
- `training_polygons.gpkg` holds the 140 training squares in the British National Grid (EPSG 27700).
